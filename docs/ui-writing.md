# Writing the app's text

How Latex4All words its labels, settings, messages and tooltips. Based on
Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (Writing) and how apps like Obsidian and
Zotero word their settings.

## Settings

- A setting is a short **name** and, only if the name isn't enough, **one
  plain sentence** saying what it does when it's on. People infer the off
  case. Obsidian: "Confirm file deletion — Ask before deleting a file."
- Descriptions are full sentences with a full stop. Labels have none.
- Sentence case everywhere ("Show line numbers", not "Show Line Numbers").
- Related settings sit in a group with a heading; groups go General,
  Writing, Research, AI, Sync.
- To send someone to another setting, link to it rather than describe its
  place, where possible.

## Words

- Plain, common words. If a word can go, it goes. Read it out loud.
- No colons or semicolons stitching ideas together ("Billed: …",
  "Unticked: …", "…; the notes stay"). Write two sentences instead.
- No invented phrases ("kept in step", "in play", "on their own", "of
  your month", "as of"). Say what happens: "updates when the vault
  changes", "automatically", "monthly quota".
- No sentences that justify themselves ("So Zotero suggests…"). Say what
  it does.
- Buttons are verbs: "Sync now", "Add", "Remove provider".
- Use "your" only where it's needed ("Favorites", not "Your favorites").
  Never "we".
- Errors say what happened and what to do, without blame or "oops":
  "Your server can't be reached. Changes are kept here and sent when it's
  back."
- "Check"/"uncheck" for checkboxes, not "tick".
